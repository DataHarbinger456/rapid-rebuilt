Part 1. These three are holding up work

Each one decides how a box on the state form gets filled for every case from here on.

Question 1 Needs your answer Mailing address and home address Section 1 of the 82040

We collect one address per customer. The form has a separate block for a mailing address and for a home address.

Question 2 Needs your answer Who certifies the VIN? Section 8 of the 82040

The form asks which authority physically verified the vehicle's VIN. Your answer decides which block gets filled and which licence, badge or office number prints on the form. This is the section the state looks at hardest, so we would rather ask than guess.

Question 3 Needs your answer Printing the certificate of title Top of page 1

The form asks whether the customer wants their new title printed, and if so whether they collect it in the office or have it mailed.

Part 2. Minor, and blank is a fine answer

These boxes stay empty on the state form unless you tell us otherwise. Skip any you are unsure about.

Question 4 Optional GVW Section 2 of the 82040

The form has two separate weight boxes, Weight and GVW. We take Weight off the title, as you confirmed on 5 August.

Question 5 Optional Odometer, 5 or 6 digits Section 6 of the 82040

The form asks how many digits the odometer display physically shows. The title never states it, so today we tick 6 for vehicles from 1995 onward and leave anything older blank.

Question 6 Optional The dealer section Section 7 of the 82040

A block for the selling dealer's sales tax registration number, dealer licence number, date of sale and amount of tax.

Question 7 Optional An unlabelled box Section 2 of the 82040

There is a box next to “Passenger” under the van use heading that the form itself does not label.

Question 8 Optional Two boxes with the same label Sections 10 and 11 of the 82040

Between the repossession section and the non-use section sit two boxes, both labelled “Full Name of Applicant”.
