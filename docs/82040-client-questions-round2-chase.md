# Client chase emails, rounds 2 and 3 — drafts, not sent

Drafted 9 Sep 2026. The round-2 questions went out 16 Aug
(`docs/82040-client-questions-gdoc.html`) and nothing has come back;
`docs/82040-client-answers.md` still holds only Genesis's round-1 answers from 13 Aug.
Ebe has answered neither round.

**Two separate emails, sent separately.** The questions form deliberately asked the two
of them independently, on the grounds that a disagreement is the useful signal. Putting
them on one thread destroys that — the second reader would see the first reader's
answer. Ebe's version is written for someone who has not answered before, so it does not
open by chasing.

Wording for questions 1 and 2 is condensed from ambiguities 2 and 6 in
`docs/84490-coverage.md`. Ambiguity 2 says an answer *likely* settles the certifier
question, so the email hedges rather than promising it. The 144 figure is the 84490's
parts table (30) plus the 84491's, which is almost nothing but that table (84 of its 122
fields) — both are blocked on the same answer.

Question 3 re-asks the VIN certifier point. Round 1's FIELD 02 came back as "varies per
inspection", which answers *when* the check is needed rather than *who* signs it, and it
described vessels on a vehicle form. The re-ask names one ordinary rebuilt car so the
answer can only be a person, and does not quote the old answer back at them.

Questions 4 to 6 are round 3, from `docs/82053-coverage.md`. They are grouped below the
first three and explicitly marked as lower priority, so a short reply still lands the
answers that unblock the most work. Question 4 folds the power-of-attorney question
together with the 82050's deferred "who is selling" question, as that audit instructs:
if the company signs the bill of sale for a customer, the POA is the legal basis, so one
answer largely settles both. Asking them separately would invite two half-answers.

Question 6 has now been deferred three times (84490 ambiguity 8, the vessels reading of
FIELD 02, and 82053 ambiguity 3). It is last because it retires fields rather than
unblocking any.

**Do not send without a read-through.** Check the sign-off name.

## Version A — Genesis

**Subject:** Three quick answers and we can finish the forms (plus three that can wait)

Hi Genesis,

Chasing the questions I sent on 16 August. Three are holding up most of the work.

**1. Is your inspector PRVIP-certified, and what agency number goes on the forms?**
The builder's form reserves a signature line for a PRVIP inspector, and we have
nothing on file to fill that number from. It should also go a long way towards
answering question 3.

**2. When a part is rebuilt or original, which box should we tick?** The state's form
offers only New, Used, Repaired, Aftermarket or Homemade. Do you ever need the last
two? We cannot record them today. Until this is settled the parts section stays blank
on both builder forms — around 144 boxes your staff fill by hand.

**3. On a normal rebuilt car titled out of state, who physically signs the line
confirming the VIN was checked?** I am after the role: your own inspector, a police
officer, a licensed dealer, a state examiner, or someone else. We print that person's
name and details, so we need to know whose.

If you only have time for three, stop there. The rest are useful but can wait.

**4. Do customers sign paperwork over to you, so you can file on their behalf? And on
the bill of sale, is your customer the buyer or the seller?** These are one question
really — if you sign for a customer, the first is what makes it legal. Getting the
buyer and seller the wrong way round is the one mistake we cannot risk, so I would
rather ask than assume.

**5. Can one signed authorisation cover two vehicles, or is it always one per job?**

**6. Do you handle anything other than cars — mobile homes, boats, boats on trailers?**
If it is cars only, a good chunk of these forms stops applying and we can hide it.

A couple of lines is plenty; I will tidy it up.

Thanks,
Ben
