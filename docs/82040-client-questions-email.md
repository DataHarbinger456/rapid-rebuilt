**Subject:** 8 quick questions on the title application form

Hi Genesis,

Good progress on the automatic form filling — the 82040 now fills itself from what
the customer submits, and I've just added the owner's contact details, the Florida
title number and the applicant name.

To fill the rest correctly I need your answers on eight boxes. I've put a suggested
default under each one, so if a default looks right just reply "default is fine"
and I'll go with it.

**These three are holding up real fields:**

**1. Mailing vs. home address.** We collect one address per customer, but the form
has separate boxes for mailing address and home address. Are they nearly always the
same for your customers, or should we start asking for both at sign-up?
*Default: treat them as the same and print one address in both blocks.*

**2. Who certifies the VIN?** Section 8 asks which authority verified the vehicle's
VIN — notary public, law enforcement, Florida dealer, FLHSMV, or tax collector.
Which one are your inspectors? This decides which block on the form gets filled and
which licence or badge number goes in it.
*No default — I can't guess this one, and it's the section the state looks at hardest.*

**3. Printing the title.** The form asks whether the customer wants their new title
printed: No, Yes in office, or Yes mailed. Is that the same answer every time, or
does the customer choose?
*Default: leave it blank for the customer to tick on the day.*

**These five are minor — a blank box is fine if you'd rather not decide now:**

**4. GVW.** The form has two separate weight boxes: Weight and GVW. We take Weight
off the title as you confirmed. Do you ever fill GVW, and if so from where?
*Default: leave GVW blank.*

**5. Odometer digits.** The form asks whether the odometer display shows 5 or 6
digits. We currently tick 6 for 1995-and-newer vehicles and leave older ones blank.
Should inspectors note this on site instead?
*Default: keep the current rule.*

**6. Dealer section.** There's a block for a selling dealer's sales tax number,
licence number, and sale amount. Does Rapid Rebuilt ever fill that in, or is it
always the dealer who sold the car?
*Default: leave the whole section blank.*

**7. An unlabelled box.** There's a box next to "Passenger" under van use that the
form doesn't label. Any idea what it's for?
*Default: leave it blank.*

**8. Two identical name boxes.** Between the repossession section and the non-use
section there are two boxes both labelled "Full Name of Applicant." Do you know
which section they belong to?
*Default: leave both blank — they only apply to repossessions.*

No rush on 4–8. If you can get me 1, 2 and 3 this week, I'll have the rest of the
owner and inspector sections filling automatically.

Thanks,
Aris
