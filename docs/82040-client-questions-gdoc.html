Florida title paperwork: thirteen questions

HSMV forms 82040 and 84490  |  Prepared for Genesis, Rapid Rebuilt  |  16 August 2026

The portal now fills the state title application automatically from what your customer submits. Eight boxes on that form need a decision from you before we can fill them safely.

Every question has a suggested answer. If the suggestion looks right, put an X next to it and move on. That is a complete answer.

Questions 1 to 3 are holding up real work. Questions 4 to 8 are minor, and leaving those boxes blank on the state form is a perfectly good outcome.

Questions 1 to 8 were answered on 13 August and are kept below for the record; you can skip straight to question 9. Questions 9 to 13 are new, about the Statement of Builder form, and question 9 is holding up work on three forms at once.

To answer: click the box next to your choice and type an X, then type into any answer box that applies. Nothing else needed.

Part 1. These three are holding up work

Each one decides how a box on the state form gets filled, for every case from here on.

Section 1 of the 82040

We collect one address per customer. The form has a separate block for a mailing address and another for a home address.

Part 3. The Statement of Builder: five new questions

Added 16 August, about the second state form in the packet. Question 9 also unlocks two of the older ones.

9. Are your inspectors PRVIP inspectors?

Section 5 of the 84490, the office page

The Statement of Builder has a signature line specifically for a "PRVIP Inspector" — an inspector licensed under the state's private inspector program — with a company or agency number printed beside their name. The portal already has a slot for that number, and it is empty.

Your answer here likely settles an older question too. Who certifies the VIN on the title application (Section 8 of the 82040) and on the verification form (Part B of the 82042) has been open since 5 August. If your inspectors are PRVIP inspectors, that is the answer to those as well, and three forms start filling themselves at once.

  Yes — our inspectors are PRVIP inspectors. The number is in the box below.
  No — we do the inspections, but not as PRVIP inspectors.
  Not sure what this is — call us and we will work it out together.

Company / agency number to print beside the inspector's name (leave blank if you answered no)

 

10. Who signs as the builder, and what is the second column for?

Section 4 of the 84490

The form is called a Statement of Builder, and it is signed under penalty of perjury by whoever rebuilt the vehicle. In your cases, is that the customer, who arranged the repair of their own car, or Rapid Rebuilt, whose shop did the work? The signature block also prints two identical name-and-address columns side by side, and the form does not say what the second one is for.

  The customer — rebuilt by owner. Print their name and address. (suggested)
  Rapid Rebuilt — we are the builder. Print the shop's name and address.
  It varies from case to case.

And the second column?

  Leave it blank. (suggested)
  It is for someone else — who, in the box below.
 

11. Four boxes marked D-1 to D-4

Section 5 of the 84490, the office page

On the office-use page sit four wide boxes labelled only D-1, D-2, D-3 and D-4, with nothing printed saying what they hold. Our inspectors already record where they found the vehicle's VIN — under the windshield, on the door jamb. Is that what these boxes are for, or do they belong to the state office?

  Don't know — leave them for the state office. (suggested)
  They are for VIN locations — print where our inspector found the VIN.
  They hold something else — what, in the box below.
 

12. The form asks for the title number twice

Section 1 of the 84490

Near the top of the form there is a "Title Number / Title State" pair, and then a second pair, "Other/Title Number / Other/Title State". We already read the salvage title's number and state off the document the customer uploads. Which pair should that print in, and does anything belong in the other one?

  The first pair. Leave the "Other" pair blank. (suggested)
  The first pair, and the "Other" pair holds the earlier out-of-state title when there was one.
  Something else — what, in the box below.
 

13. The parts list offers different condition choices

Section 2 of the 84490

In the portal, customers record each replacement part as new, used, repaired, rebuilt or original. On the state form the columns are New, Used, Repaired, Aftermarket and Homemade. So "rebuilt" and "original" parts have no column to print in, and "aftermarket" and "homemade" parts cannot be recorded in the portal at all.

  Treat rebuilt as Repaired and original as Used, and add Aftermarket and Homemade as choices in the portal. (suggested)
  Treat both rebuilt and original as Repaired, and add the two portal choices.
  Handle it differently — how, in the box below.
