## The problem we're solving

Right now the rebuilt-title process runs on paper, phone calls, and memory.
Customers show up with the wrong documents. Forms get filled by hand, and one
transposed VIN digit means a rejected filing and a wasted appointment.
Nobody knows where their case stands without calling and asking.

Every one of those is a leak — of time, of money, of reputation.

**The fix:** get the paperwork in *before* inspection day, verified and complete,
with the state forms already filled out. Day-of becomes print, wet-sign, inspect, done.

## Stage 2 — The part nobody else has

**Upload a photo of a salvage title. Watch the state form fill itself in.**

This is the edge. No other rebuilt-title shop in Florida is doing it.

- **AI document reading** — customer photographs their title, bill of sale, ID, and
  receipts. The system reads them and extracts the owner, VIN, dates, and amounts.
- **Human verification** — your staff sees what the AI read side-by-side with the
  original document and confirms or corrects it. Nothing reaches a state form unverified.
- **Official Florida HSMV forms filled automatically** — 82040, 84490, 84491, 82050,
  82053, and 82042. The real state PDFs, not homemade lookalikes. This matters if you
  ever license this or approach Tallahassee.
- **Readiness meter** — "4 of 6 documents approved, receipt missing donor VIN."
  Green light before inspection day means no more wasted appointments.
- **Guided phone capture** — opens the customer's camera, catches blurry or
  cut-off photos before they submit.
- **One-click day-of packet** — every form printed, labeled, ready for wet signature.

**What you'll see:** a phone photo of a salvage title goes in, a completed 82040 comes out.
